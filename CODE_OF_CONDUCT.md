# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). What follows is a short summary; the linked text is the reference.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; focus on the technical question.
- Assume good faith, and ask for evidence instead of assuming.
- Respect that people write in English or Portuguese, and that not everyone is a native speaker.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Public or private harassment, or publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@dougllascda](https://github.com/dougllascda)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions, or restrict participation, when conduct is inappropriate. This applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

To report a problem, open an issue (without sensitive details) asking for a private channel, or contact [@dougllascda](https://github.com/dougllascda) on GitHub. Reports are handled with discretion.
